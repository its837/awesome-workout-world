What is this?

Awesome Workout World is the platform: a home screen for the Peloton bike or treadmill in your basement. No membership needed. It is yours: you decide what is on it.

Merry Miles-a-thon is the first game on it. Walk or ride one mile a day from Thanksgiving to Christmas. Every day you do, a door on the advent calendar opens, and something comes out of it and joins your walks. A turkey. A penguin. Your dog. Your dog's poop. Your dead uncle.

It reads your real speed and incline off the machine, so the little you on the screen walks when you walk.

It only reads. It cannot start, stop or speed up a belt, and there is no code in it that could.

Put it on your Peloton

You need a computer, a USB cable that carries data, and a nerd (or an AI that is willing to be one). The short version:

  1. POINT YOUR CLAUDE HEREOpen it in the folder of this project. Say: read the file for stupid people, and take me through it.
  2. PLUG STUFF INA USB cable from your computer to the tablet. Turn on USB debugging. It will tell you where.
  3. CUSTOMIZE ITA family meeting asks about your people, pets and weird stupid in-jokes. Or go rogue and tell it your dreams.
  4. GO NUTSIt builds the app and puts it on the tablet. You walk. The tablet is yours.
  5. GOOD LUCKIt might break. Every step comes with how to undo it.

SIDE BY SIDE

  • One more app on the tablet.

  • Uninstall it and it is gone.

FULL TAKEOVER

  • The tablet starts up into Awesome Workout World.
  • Peloton becomes one of the apps.
  • You choose what else is on the home page.
  • For you if the Peloton app is dead to you.

By default it is just the game and an ALL APPS page. If you want video or music apps on the home page, you add an app store to the tablet yourself first (the install guide says how); a Peloton has none. Both ways are written out step by step, with how to undo each step, in the install guide. What has been tried on which machine is in the bike and treadmill notes. And there is a file for stupid people using AI to do things they shouldn't. Read it first.

Have a family meeting, or go rogue and bring your family’s dreams to life.

Your AI asks. You answer, like at the dinner table. Your people, your pets, the family who are not here any more, what you eat, the weird stupid stuff. About twenty minutes. It fills in the blanks the game already has.

GO ROGUE

Both start with your AI telling you that you are awesome, which is true. Every door can be about your family. The game is the frame; you are the picture. Here is the whole calendar, a day at a time, with something to try on each:

    The more personal, the better. That is what is beautiful about the future.

    FACESEverybody is drawn from your answers: height, hair, glasses, a hat, a jacket colour.
    PETSDogs run beside you and earn miles. Every other pet gets a line, because only dogs can be drawn.
